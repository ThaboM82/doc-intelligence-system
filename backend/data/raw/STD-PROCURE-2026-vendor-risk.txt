ACME CORP — PROCUREMENT STANDARD
Document ID: STD-PROCURE-2026-VR
Title: Third-Party Vendor Risk for Cloud AI Services
Classification: Internal
Effective: 2026-01-20
Owner: Procurement & Security

1. PURPOSE
Define due diligence requirements before adopting third-party AI, embedding,
or vector database services that process ACME data.

2. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Signed data processing agreement (DPA) addressing subprocessors.
2.2 Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest for stored customer content.
2.3 Documented retention and deletion procedures compatible with POL-INFOSEC-2026-DR.
2.4 Support for API key rotation and least-privilege roles.
2.5 Security incident notification to ACME within seventy-two (72) hours of
    confirmed breach affecting ACME data.

3. PROHIBITED USES
3.1 Sending regulated health or payment card data to models without prior
    Legal and CISO approval.
3.2 Training public models on confidential ACME documents without contract
    terms that prohibit provider training use.

4. APPROVED CATEGORIES (EXAMPLES)
- Hosted vector databases with VPC or IP allowlisting options
- Enterprise LLM APIs with no-training contractual clauses
- Document parsing services under active DPA

5. REVIEW CADENCE
Material vendors are reassessed annually or upon major product change.
