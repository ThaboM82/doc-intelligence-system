ACME CORP — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Document ID: POL-INFOSEC-2026-DR
Title: Data Retention and Disposal
Classification: Internal
Effective: 2026-01-15
Owner: Chief Information Security Officer

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines minimum and maximum retention periods for business records
and the approved methods for secure disposal.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all employees, contractors, and systems that store ACME customer or
employee data, including cloud object storage, databases, and the Document
Intelligence platform.

3. RETENTION PERIODS
3.1 Customer account records: retained for seven (7) years after account closure
    unless a shorter period is required by a written customer contract.
3.2 Financial invoices and ledgers: retained for ten (10) years.
3.3 Security incident logs: retained for three (3) years minimum; critical
    incident packages retained for seven (7) years.
3.4 Employee HR files: retained for seven (7) years after termination.
3.5 Marketing analytics events: retained for twenty-four (24) months.

4. LEGAL HOLD
When Legal issues a litigation hold, automatic deletion is suspended for all
in-scope records until the hold is released in writing.

5. DISPOSAL
5.1 Digital media must be wiped using approved cryptographic erasure or
    physically destroyed via certified vendor.
5.2 Paper records must be cross-cut shredded; certificates of destruction
    retained for two (2) years.

6. EXCEPTIONS
Exceptions require written approval from CISO and Legal and must be logged
in the compliance register within five (5) business days.

7. ENFORCEMENT
Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination
and, where applicable, regulatory notification.
